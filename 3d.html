3D WORK

After taking your measurements, they are transferred into a 3D program, where a digital avatar is created based on your body proportions. This means that you can see what the jeans will look like before they are made, which helps to avoid wasting fabric and reprinting.

The 3D version also makes it easier to see how the jeans might fit a real person. But it's still just a digital copy and can't completely replace the physical fitting process. Once the pattern is printed and we start sewing, further adjustments can be made and details will be added to get the best fit.

by Benjamin Scott
